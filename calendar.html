Academic year

📅 Academic calendar

Three terms a year, with a short break between each. Interview slots for new applicants are booked within Term dates below.

JAN – MAR

Term 1

Classes resume for the new academic year.

  • Orientation week for new students
  • Mid-term assessments — late February
  • Term 1 break — last week of March
MAY – JUL

Term 2

The longest term of the year, including sports week.

  • Inter-house sports week — early June
  • Mid-term assessments — mid-June
  • Term 2 break — last week of July
SEP – NOV

Term 3

Closes the academic year with final examinations.

  • Mid-term assessments — mid-October
  • Final examinations — early November
  • Prize-giving day & year close — late November
🗓️

Booking an admissions interview?

Interview slots open two weeks before each term begins. Sign in to the portal to see live availability and reserve a time.
