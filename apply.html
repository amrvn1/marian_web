Admissions

📄 Apply for admission

Four steps from interest to enrolment. Applications for the next intake at Marian Schools, Kerege, are reviewed on a rolling basis.

How it works

The application process

Submit your details

Sign in to the student & parent portal and complete the applicant form with the student's name, date of birth and current school.

Upload documents

Attach the most recent report card, a birth certificate, and one passport photo. Files are reviewed by the admissions office within five working days.

Book an interview

Once documents are reviewed, choose a slot on the academic calendar for a short interview with the applicant and a parent or guardian.

Confirmation & enrolment

Successful applicants receive a confirmation letter and a place in the coming term, along with a checklist for uniforms and orientation day.
